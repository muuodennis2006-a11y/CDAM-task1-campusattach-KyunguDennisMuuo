## Project Overview

CampusAttach is a proposed web-based platform designed to help university students discover and manage attachment and internship opportunities in a more organized way.

The project was motivated by a real problem observed during my university studies. Over the past three years, I have seen my brother and friends struggle to find suitable attachment and internship opportunities. Information about opportunities is often shared through different channels, making it difficult for students to discover relevant opportunities, understand requirements and deadlines, and keep track of their applications.

I will also need to search for an attachment opportunity during my studies. This personal connection to the problem motivated me to explore a software-based solution that can make the process of finding and managing opportunities more organized.

## Problem Statement

University students often struggle to find suitable attachment and internship opportunities because information is distributed across different sources such as social media platforms, university notices, personal networks, organization websites, and other online channels.

This can make it difficult for students to find relevant opportunities, understand requirements and deadlines, and keep track of applications.

CampusAttach proposes a centralized platform where students can discover opportunities, view their details, apply where applicable, and track their application progress.

## Objectives

The main objective of CampusAttach is to develop a web-based platform that helps university students discover and manage attachment and internship opportunities while providing organizations with a structured way to publish and manage opportunities.

Specific objectives include:

- Provide students with accounts and profiles.
- Allow students to browse attachment and internship opportunities.
- Provide search and filtering functionality.
- Allow students to view opportunity details and requirements.
- Allow students to apply for available opportunities.
- Allow students to track application status.
- Allow organizations to create and manage opportunity listings.
- Allow organizations to review applications and update application status.
- Provide administrators with basic user and opportunity moderation functionality.
